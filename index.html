How scoring and categories work

Each round is worth up to 5,000 points: 2,000 for naming the map and 3,000 for the pin. A pin within 50 pixels of the crop's centre (measured on the full-size panorama) gets full marks; beyond that the points fall off with distance. Both parts are scaled by a multiplier that drops each time you zoom the crop out (×1.00, ×0.85, ×0.70, ×0.55, ×0.40) and, if the time penalty is on, by up to 25% over the 90 seconds before you lock in a name. A wrong name ends the round with no points.

The built-in categories load their panoramas from the MO and RA2YR folders next to this file, so keep the three together. “Custom folder” plays any other folder of images: each image's name is its filename, or whatever a names.json in that folder maps it to.
